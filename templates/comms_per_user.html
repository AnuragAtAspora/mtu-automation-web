Communications Per User Calculator

Calculate the average number of communications (transactional and promotional) received per user for UK and UAE markets. This metric helps understand communication frequency and user engagement patterns.

Select the last day of your analysis period. The system will analyze from the 1st of that month to your selected date.
What This Calculator Does:

Downloads 4 Reports:

  • Transactional Push Notifications
  • Transactional Emails
  • Promotional Push Notifications
  • Promotional Emails

Calculates Metrics:

  • Promotional: Total sent ÷ Total users
  • Transactional: Total sent ÷ Users who transacted
  • Separate results for UK and UAE
  • Automatic Google Sheets update
Processing Time:

This process downloads and analyzes 4 separate campaign reports. It may take 30-60 seconds to complete. Please be patient after clicking Calculate.

How It Works
1. Report Generation

System generates report filenames based on your selected date and downloads them via MoEngage Reports API.

2. Country Filtering

Campaigns are automatically filtered by country based on campaign names containing "UK" or "UAE".

3. User Base Calculation

Uses existing user segments to determine total users and users who transacted in the period.

4. Results & Export

Displays detailed metrics and automatically updates your Google Sheets with the results.
